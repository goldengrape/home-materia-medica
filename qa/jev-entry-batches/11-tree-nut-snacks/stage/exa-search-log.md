## Selected findings and screening decisions

- **Macadamia:** selected the 2023 randomized crossover trial (DOI 10.1017/jns.2023.39). Kept null between-period results and its 35-person, 8-week-per-period scope; excluded oil and unrelated nut evidence.
- **Hazelnut:** retained the 2016 Bayesian meta-analysis (DOI 10.3390/nu8120747), 2022 systematic review (DOI 10.3390/ijerph19052880), and 2016 raw versus roasted/lightly salted trial (DOI 10.1007/s00394-015-1150-4). The latter bundles roasting and salt. The Bencao Gangmu “榛” name is historical evidence with unresolved modern species identity.
- **Brazil nut:** kept the 10-person acute trial (DOI 10.1155/2013/653185), the randomized-trial meta-analysis (DOI 10.3390/antiox11020403), the 91-person partially defatted granulate study (DOI 10.1186/s12937-015-0043-y), and NIH ODS selenium references. Do not treat granulate as whole kernels or infer an individualized dose.
- **Pine kernels:** direct whole-kernel human evidence was not located. Korean pine oil and triacylglycerol papers (DOIs 10.1186/1476-511X-7-6 and 10.1186/1743-7075-8-79) remain labeled as oil/extract evidence. The historical “海松子” source distinguishes a named foreign pine and is not generalized to all Pinus.
- **Salted nuts:** selected the 72-person hazelnut processing trial only as a single-product comparison; salt cannot be separated from roasting. No direct category-wide clinical trial found.
- **Honey-roasted nuts:** no direct tree-nut product trial located. The 2014 peanut flavoring trial (DOI 10.3945/ajcn.113.069401) is retained as adjacent evidence only; peanuts are legumes.
- **Sugar-coated nuts:** recorded USDA FDC 170656 as one sugar-coated almond sample (465 kcal and 62.5 g sugars per 100 g); not a category mean or clinical study.
- **Cocoa-coated nuts:** the 2017 controlled-feeding trial (DOI 10.1161/JAHA.116.005162) compares almonds and cocoa/dark chocolate treatments; it is not a coated-nut product test.
- **Spicy nuts:** no direct tree-nut product trial located; isolated capsaicin or chili studies were screened out as non-equivalent exposures.
- **Mixed nuts:** retained 2019 and 2023 fixed-recipe replacement trials (DOIs 10.3390/nu11071488 and 10.1016/j.numecd.2023.05.013); preserve their small samples, specific recipes, surrogate outcomes, and isocaloric pretzel comparators.

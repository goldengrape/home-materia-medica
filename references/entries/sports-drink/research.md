## 1. 身份与边界

**本条定义：** 为运动期间补充水、碳水化合物和电解质而设计的饮料；与口服补液盐和普通电解质水的配方目标不同。

**主要变量：** 碳水浓度与类型、钠含量、渗透压、摄入速率、运动持续时间/强度、环境温度、出汗量和运动者个体差异。

品牌、地区、加工、容量及配方会改变实际暴露；本底稿只讨论该目录类别的合理边界。邻近饮料或单一原料的研究不会自动视作本成品证据。

## 4. 传统来源层级

本条是现代加工或复合饮料。本轮未发现足以支持其特定配方与古籍条目一一对应的直接材料；传统食疗记录不转写为现代产品的药性或疗效。

## 5. 组成与暴露

有等渗、低渗或高渗等产品，碳水和钠含量差异较大；“等渗”名称不保证实测渗透压或临床效果。

决定实际暴露的项目：碳水浓度与类型、钠含量、渗透压、摄入速率、运动持续时间/强度、环境温度、出汗量和运动者个体差异。

## 6. 本条证据卡

### 命题 A｜成品人体结局

运动营养共识支持在特定持续时间与强度的训练/竞赛中安排碳水和水分摄入；28 项研究的荟萃分析考察运动中不同渗透压运动饮料与水对血浆容量变化的影响。这些结果限于运动过程的补水/表现情境，不证明日常饮用更健康（运动情境 E-B；一般人群长期获益 E-0）。

### 命题 B｜产品、加工或成分

运动饮料通常碳水含量高于口服补液盐、钠含量低于医疗 ORS。含糖、酸味运动饮料长期频繁含漱/啜饮也会增加口腔糖酸暴露。

### Evidence card 1 — source 1

- **Design:** Professional joint position statement. 
- **Population:** Athletes across training/competition scenarios.
- **Exposure:** Food, fluids and supplements including sport beverage use.
- **Result:** Provides situational guidance for type, amount and timing of nutrition/hydration to support performance and recovery.
- **Limits:** Guideline for sport performance; not evidence that all consumers benefit from routine sports drinks.
- **Distance:** D1
- **Reference:** Thomas DT, Erdman KA, Burke LM. “Position of the Academy of Nutrition and Dietetics, Dietitians of Canada, and the American College of Sports Medicine: Nutrition and Athletic Performance.” Journal of the Academy of Nutrition and Dietetics. 2016;116(3):501–528. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jand.2015.12.006.

### Evidence card 2 — source 2

- **Design:** Systematic review and random-effects meta-analysis; 28 studies and 68 drink treatment effects.
- **Population:** Participants in continuous exercise studies.
- **Exposure:** Hypertonic, isotonic, hypotonic carbohydrate-electrolyte drinks and water over 0–180 minutes.
- **Result:** Estimated central hydration/plasma volume effects varied by drink tonicity and intake conditions.
- **Limits:** Surrogate plasma-volume outcome; beverages and exercise protocols varied; does not establish long-term health effects.
- **Distance:** D0/D1
- **Reference:** Kopetschny BH, Badenhorst CE. “The Hydrating Effects of Hypertonic, Isotonic and Hypotonic Sports Drinks and Waters on Central Hydration During Continuous Exercise: A Systematic Meta-Analysis and Perspective.” Sports Medicine. 2022;52(2):349–375. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1007/s40279-021-01558-y.

### Evidence card 3 — source 3

- **Design:** Systematic review of controlled trials for exercise-associated dehydration.
- **Population:** Adults and children dehydrated after exercise.
- **Exposure:** Carbohydrate-electrolyte solution compared with water.
- **Result:** Reviewed evidence on rehydration effectiveness and beverage type.
- **Limits:** Exercise-related dehydration, not diarrhea ORS or routine daily hydration.
- **Distance:** D0/D1
- **Reference:** Borra V, De Brier N, Berry D, Zideman D, Singletary EM, De Buck E, et al. “Oral Rehydration Beverages for Treating Exercise-Associated Dehydration: A Systematic Review, Part I. Carbohydrate-Electrolyte Solutions.” Journal of Athletic Training. 2023. DOI: https://doi.org/10.4085/1062-6050-0682.22.


样品、产地、品牌、剂量、研究时间、比较组和终点不得超出原研究外推；体外或标签分析不升级为人群效应。若原文报告利益冲突或产品/菌株特异性，按限制项保留。

## 7. 安全与实用选择

日常轻活动通常不需额外含糖运动饮料；长时间、高强度运动及高温情境应按个体出汗、碳水和钠需求选择。口腔健康关注频繁接触糖与酸。

以上为类别层面的阅读提示，不替代个体医疗建议；不把分类名、营销词或单一研究结果当成疗效承诺。
